# Product Context: FatooraBot

## Why This Project Exists
Manual invoice creation is time-consuming and error-prone. FatooraBot aims to streamline this through a conversational bot interface.

## Problems It Solves
- Manual invoice generation overhead
- Inconsistent invoice formatting
- Lack of automation in small business invoicing workflows

## Target Users
- Small business owners
- Freelancers
- Accountants managing multiple clients

## User Experience Goals
- Simple conversational interface to create invoices
- Quick generation with minimal input
- PDF/printable output
- Invoice history and tracking
